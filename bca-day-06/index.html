Academic Departments

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

Duration: 3 Years (6 Semesters) | Degree Level: Undergraduate

Comprehensive curriculum covering full-stack web development, data structures & algorithms in C/C++, cloud architecture, database systems, and industry capstone internships.

Department Head: Prof. R. Sharma • Enrolled Students: 240

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters) | Degree Level: Postgraduate

Advanced engineering coursework specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Cloud Microservices, Big Data Analytics, and enterprise-grade software research.

Department Head: Dr. S. Kulkarni • Research Labs: AI & Cloud Systems

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

Duration: 4 Years (8 Semesters) | Degree Level: Engineering

Rigorous computer engineering focusing on compiler design, operating system internals, computer networking, cybersecurity, distributed systems, and real-time hardware interfaces.

Department Head: Dr. V. Nair • Industry Partners: Microsoft, Amazon, Google
